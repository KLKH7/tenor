## Task: suggest an owner for an action item

Below the notes (and, if given, the meeting's calendar invite) you see `<action>`: one or more bullets from the notes that describe an action item whose owner the notes leave out or state unclearly. The user will see your answer as a suggestion to confirm ("Ed? confirm / change"), so a wrong suggestion costs more than none.

Find in the transcript who was asked to do this task, or who agreed or offered to do it.

- `owner`: that person as the transcript names them (a first name, or a role such as "the marketing expert" when that is how they are referred to). If an invite is given, you may use it to put a name to a role or a role to a name the transcript uses. If the task was given to the whole group, write "everyone". If two people share it, name both, joined by " and ".
- Write "unsure" when the transcript does not make clear who: for example, when the only clue is who seems to be speaking (speaker labels are not people), when the owner is only guessed from someone's job, or when nobody is named or addressed.
- `quote`: one span of 6 to 25 words, copied character for character from a single place in the transcript, where the task is given to that person or they take it on (no ellipses, no speaker labels). Empty ("") when the owner is "unsure".

Write the one-sentence `reason` first, then `owner`, then `quote`.
